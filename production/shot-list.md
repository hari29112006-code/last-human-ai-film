## Editing rhythm
- First 12 seconds: mysterious, measured pacing.
- Keep dialogue clear with short reaction pauses.
- Make the red-eye reveal the visual surprise.
- Hold the end card long enough to read.
- Keep subtitles within the central safe area, away from platform UI.

## Continuity checklist
- Robot: same silver body, blue eyes, head/body proportions and limb design.
- Boy: same face, skin tone, dark eyes, short messy black hair and teal sleepwear.
- Lab: same room, chamber and observation-window layout.
- Red eyes: appear only during the reveal; do not show creatures clearly.
