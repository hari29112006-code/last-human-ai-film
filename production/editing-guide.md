# Editing, sound design and export guide

## Voice direction
- **Boy:** Young, natural, sleepy and confused. “Where is everyone?” is quiet and uncertain. “What are they?” is a whisper.
- **Robot:** Calm synthetic voice with warmth and sadness. Deliver “Humans disappeared 100 years ago... except you.” slowly, pausing before “except you.”
- Keep dialogue clear; duck music under speech.

## Sound design
- 00:00–00:06: low atmospheric sci-fi drone, distant electrical crackle, metallic footsteps and servo sounds.
- 00:06–00:12: chamber hum, scanner beep, rising power-up, glass seal hiss.
- 00:12–00:18: reduce music under dialogue; faint room tone.
- 00:18–00:21: build a dark low-frequency pulse and faint mechanical clicking.
- 00:21–00:23: short metallic impact / bass hit, then abrupt silence on black.
- 00:23–00:25: one ominous pulse with short reverb tail.

Use original, licensed or royalty-free audio only.

## Subtitle styling
Clean bold sans-serif, white text with subtle dark shadow or semi-transparent dark backing. One or two lines at a time. Place in lower-middle safe area, not flush to bottom. Check spelling and timing manually.

## Export settings
- Container: MP4
- Video: H.264
- Resolution: 1080 × 1920
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Frame rate: match timeline (24 or 30 fps)
- Audio: AAC, 48 kHz
- Duration: 25 seconds
- No watermark or platform logo in rendered video.

## Quality-control checklist
- [ ] Boy face, hairstyle and teal outfit match in every shot.
- [ ] Robot silver body and blue eyes remain identical.
- [ ] Lab layout is consistent.
- [ ] Dialogue is audible and subtitles correctly timed.
- [ ] Red eyes appear only at reveal.
- [ ] End card is readable on a phone.
- [ ] No accidental logos, watermarks or generated gibberish.
- [ ] Watch full export once before publishing.
