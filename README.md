# THE LAST HUMAN — PART 1

A 25-second vertical cinematic 3D animated sci-fi mystery for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

**Creator:** @AU_HARI_  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 1080 × 1920 · 25 seconds · 24 or 30 fps  
**Genre:** Sci-fi · mystery · suspense

## Logline
In 2099, a small exploration robot discovers a sleeping human boy in an abandoned underground laboratory—only to realise that something else is watching them.

## Character continuity
**Robot:** Small compact brushed-silver exploration robot, rounded body, expressive glowing blue eyes, small articulated arms and legs. Keep exact proportions and eye design in every shot.

**Boy:** Young boy around 10, warm medium-brown skin, soft oval face, large dark-brown eyes, short slightly messy black hair, teal futuristic sleepwear with a simple white chest detail. Keep face, hairstyle and outfit identical throughout.

## Production workflow
1. Generate clean reference images for the robot and boy.
2. Generate each shot separately, reusing the same references and fixed seed/reference settings where available.
3. Generate or record dialogue and sound effects.
4. Assemble clips, subtitles, music and end cards in a video editor.
5. Export and review the 1080 × 1920 MP4.

**Note:** This repository contains production assets and prompts, not a rendered video. A separate video-generation/editor tool is needed to render the finished animation.
